# Code of conduct

This policy applies to BatteryBridge issues, pull requests, reviews and other
project spaces, including interactions where someone represents the project.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work and its evidence. Critique ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Be respectful across backgrounds, identities, abilities and experience levels.
- Accept constructive feedback and respect another person's boundaries.
- Keep reports useful: share reproducible information and protect personal data.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, doxxing, spam
and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish someone else's
private information or repeat unwanted contact.

## Reporting and enforcement

The repository owner, [@0then0](https://github.com/0then0), handles conduct reports.
If you do not already have a private contact channel, request one in an issue
without naming affected people or publishing the incident's details. Do not
use a public thread to disclose sensitive information.

Maintainers consider the report's context and impact. They may remove content,
request a change in behavior, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation
or block an account. Severe threats or repeated abuse may lead to an immediate
restriction. Maintainers should explain decisions privately where possible and
limit disclosure of a reporter's identity to what is necessary to handle the report.

To ask for reconsideration, contact the repository owner through the same private
channel and explain the relevant facts. Good-faith reports must not lead to retaliation.
